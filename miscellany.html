  • I contributed to the Japanese translations of Jeff Weeks's Curved Spaces and KaleidoPaint, both free. (See here.) I admire how clearly he explains geometric ideas. I first heard about his book The Shape of Space, which builds on Edwin Abbott's Flatland, in Lisa Piccirillo's Talk at Google. I read it towards the end of undergrad and fell in love with 3-manifold topology, and for a while I was interested in the topology of the physical universe. Curved Spaces lets you fly through universes with different 3-manifold topologies.
